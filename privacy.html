Good to know / 01

Your privacy
is important.

This draft explains the kinds of information that may be handled when you browse this website or contact TREVGO.

Last updated: 18 October 2026. This is a starter policy for the pre-launch website and must be checked against the actual business, hosting, analytics and contact-form setup before publication.

1. Who is responsible

TREVGO (“we”, “us” or “our”) is preparing a ride-booking platform. Before launch, this page should identify the legal entity responsible for the website, its registered or business address, and a monitored privacy contact.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us or submit an enquiry, we may handle information such as your name, email address, the subject of the enquiry and the message you provide. Avoid sending passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information through a general enquiry form.

3. Technical information

Our hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as browser requests, IP addresses, timestamps and error logs. Any analytics, advertising pixels, cookies or third-party services should be listed here if they are enabled.

4. How information may be used

Contact details may be used to respond to enquiries, manage launch-interest requests, maintain website security and meet applicable legal obligations. We should not use contact information for unrelated marketing without a valid legal basis and any required consent.

5. Service providers and international transfers

Website hosting, email, analytics and embedded media providers may process information on our behalf or under their own terms. Before launch, this section should identify relevant providers and explain any international data transfers that apply.

6. Retention and your choices

We intend to keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected or as required by law. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or object to certain uses of your information. Contact the privacy address published by the responsible legal entity to make a request.

7. Children and changes

This website is not intended to invite children to submit personal information. We may update this notice when the website or service changes and will revise the date above.

Before using this as a live policy, confirm the legal entity, contact details, hosting setup, cookies and analytics, retention practice and applicable jurisdiction. Do not publish unverified details.
