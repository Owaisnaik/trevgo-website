These draft website terms set out the limits of this pre-launch information site.

Last updated: 18 October 2026. These terms are a starter draft and should be reviewed by an appropriate professional before publication.

1. Website purpose

This website provides information about TREVGO and a planned ride-booking experience. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the website does not allow you to book a ride, obtain a confirmed fare, or enter into a ride-service contract.

2. Planned services

Bike, auto and taxi are planned ride categories. Service areas, prices, availability, eligibility, partner onboarding and launch timing may change and will be confirmed only through official service communications.

3. Website content

We make reasonable efforts to keep the website accurate, but pre-launch descriptions may change and are provided for general information. Visuals or video may be illustrative and should not be read as a promise that a particular vehicle, route, service area or feature is available.

4. Acceptable use

You agree not to misuse the website, attempt to interfere with its security or availability, introduce malicious code, or use its content unlawfully.

5. Third-party services

The website may link to services or media operated by third parties. We do not control those services and their terms and privacy policies apply to your use of them.

6. Limitation and updates

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the website is provided for general information and may be changed or made unavailable without notice. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot lawfully be limited.

7. Contact and governing law

Before launch, complete this section with TREVGO’s legal business name, service address, contact details and the governing-law and dispute-resolution language approved for the operating business.

Do not treat this draft as a final service agreement. Ride bookings, payments, cancellations, refunds and driver-partner terms require service-specific terms once the product is live.
