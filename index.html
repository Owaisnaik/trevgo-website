How we think about safety ↗

  • Built around real journeys

    One place to choose a bike, auto or taxi, with the steps before a trip made easy to understand.

  • Clear before you go

    Ride type, journey details and service information should be clear before a booking is confirmed.

  • Help you can find

    Passengers and driver-partners should know where to look for help. Support routes will be published once the service is ready.

  • A careful start

    TREVGO may launch in stages, depending on the area. We’ll share details before service begins.
