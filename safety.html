People first

Moving with
more care.

Feeling looked after starts with knowing what to expect. We’re keeping that in mind as we build TREVGO.

Safety is not a badge or a single feature. It needs to be considered throughout the ride experience, from the information shown before a trip to the support available when something goes wrong.

Clarity before a ride

TREVGO intends to make ride type, journey details and service information clear before a booking is confirmed. The final in-app information and features will be described before the service launches.

Help when you need it

Passengers and driver-partners should know where to look for help. The support routes, escalation steps and operating procedures will be published once the service and its support operations are ready.

A careful start

TREVGO may launch in stages. Which rides are offered and when will depend on the area. We’ll share details before service begins.

This page describes TREVGO’s approach and intentions. It does not claim that any particular safety tool, emergency-response system or live ride service is currently available. Please review and update this page to match the actual product before launch.
