A little about us

Movement is
part of living.

Getting around should feel like the easy part of the day. That’s the idea behind TREVGO.

A quick dash across town. A familiar auto ride. A taxi when you need more room. Different days call for different ways to get there.

Built around real journeys

We’re working towards one place to choose a bike, auto or taxi. The aim is to make the steps before a trip easy to understand, without making you think harder than you need to.

People are the point

A good ride experience starts with people. We want passengers and driver-partners to have the information they need and a clear idea of what to expect.

Thoughtful by design

For us, good design means making choices feel clear, sharing useful information at the right time and treating safety and trust as work that never stops.

We’ll share service areas, fares and launch timing when they’re confirmed. You can’t book a ride through this website yet.
